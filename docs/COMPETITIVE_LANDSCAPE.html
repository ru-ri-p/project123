The competitive landscape, and where Attest can actually win

A researched map of who is building tamper-evident AI audit and AI-compliance tooling — globally and in the UAE — how each compares to Attest, and a candid assessment of which of our advantages are real moats versus fast-eroding table stakes.

Method Four parallel web-research passes, sourced & dated As of 2026-09-05 Confidence High on structure; verify individual figures
The one thing to take away. Attest's cryptography is no longer a differentiator — RFC-3161 anchoring, Ed25519 signing, hash-chains and Merkle proofs are now open-source, standardised (IETF SCITT), and appearing on a dozen 2025–26 landing pages. But the combination Attest ships — an inline compliance gate and an externally-anchored, independently-verifiable record and customer-held-key content encryption, aimed at UAE regulated finance — was found nowhere else. The moat is not the hash chain. It is the bundle, the insider-proof trust model, and the jurisdiction.

01 · Demand engine

The regulation creating the need is real and dated

Three converging regimes turn "provable AI records" from a nice-to-have into a compliance obligation. Two are UAE-specific and live now; one is the global bar-setter.

DIFC Reg 10
Full enforcement from 1 Jan 2026: AI registers, accountability, document-before-processing, Autonomous Systems Officer. The world's first AI-specific data regulation.
CBUAE AI/ML
Guidance (Feb 2026): model inventory, continuous monitoring, human oversight, Board accountability — no third-party/vendor carve-out.
DFSA records
Baseline: retain ≥6 years, reproducible in English within 3 days. A natural home for a tamper-evident ledger.
EU AI Act Art. 12
Enforceable 2 Aug 2026: automatic, tamper-evident, traceable logs for high-risk AI — read by commentators as needing independent qualified timestamps.
The honest catch: no UAE instrument yet mandates cryptographic tamper-evidence or external anchoring specifically. The rules require records that are accountable, retained and reproducible — which ordinary (mutable) GRC logging can nominally satisfy. Attest sells a "provable / insider-proof" upgrade ahead of the letter of the rules. That is both the opportunity (be there first) and the core sales challenge (teach why immutable-and-anchored beats good-enough logging before a regulator or an incident forces it).

02 · The field

Who is building what

The market splits into camps that each own one of Attest's pillars. Almost nobody owns two; nobody found owns all of them.

Direct crypto-audit micro-clusterHead-to-head

The real frontier competition, and the biggest surprise of this research: a cluster of 2025–26 entrants shipping almost exactly Attest's architecture (Ed25519 + hash-chain + Merkle + RFC-3161 anchor + offline verify).

Attested Intelligence (AGA) — nearly feature-identical, aimed at AI-agent governance, npm-installable, open verifier. VeritasChain (VCP) — same primitives, filed an IETF SCITT profile, trading/Japan focus. Senthex — firewall + anchored audit + post-quantum signing (matches our algorithm-ID foresight). DeepInspect (Dec 2025) — inline gate + tamper-evident store, but HMAC (symmetric, not independently verifiable), CISO market. Plus Soma, NexArt, SteelSpine, Vaara, KYDE, wirken (mostly open-source / pre-seed, scale unproven).
"System of record" archiving incumbentsMessaging collision

They already own the words "tamper-evident system of record" for regulated finance — via WORM storage + vendor-attested integrity checks. Crucially: that still requires trusting the vendor, gives no portable third-party proof, and does not defend against a compromised vendor. And none of them gates AI output.

Global Relay, Smarsh (WORM, SEC 17a-4), Behavox, Theta Lake (leaning hardest into "AI communications governance"), Shield, Relativity. The most important narrative battle Attest must win: WORM ≠ externally-anchored, independently-verifiable, insider-proof.
FS RegTech — AI-output complianceDirect (positioning)

These check AI / comms content against regulatory rules — Attest's gate half — but their record layer is an ordinary, mutable audit log.

Norm Ai — best-funded by far (~$267M raised, ~$1.2B val, >$30T AUM client base); encodes regs as decision trees, supervises other AI systems' outputs. Sedric — real-time comms/marketing compliance. Saifr (Fidelity) — FS marketing-compliance review, shipping into Microsoft Azure AI Foundry. None claims cryptographic tamper-evidence.
Real-time guardrails / AI securityGate only

Genuine inline output blocking — but no anchored record; it's AI security, not audit provenance.

Arthur (Shield) — "firewall for LLMs". Cisco AI Defense (ex-Robust Intelligence, ~$400M acq). Azure AI Content Safety, Fiddler ($100M raised).
Enterprise AI-governance platformsBudget incumbents

Sell governance workflow + documentation. Their "immutable audit log" is a database/retention claim, not crypto-grade. They own the C-suite "responsible AI" budget line Attest must not get lost inside.

IBM watsonx.governance — the UAE incumbent to beat (live deployment at telco e&, deep MENA/FS relationships). Credo AI (~$41M) — markets an "immutable audit log" that is DB-grade. OneTrust, Collibra, ServiceNow, Modulos, Trustible, ModelOp, and Monitaur — the one incumbent that may do cryptographically-signed decision logs (unverified on its own site) and whose FS/insurance buyer overlaps Attest most.
Adjacent — not competitorsDifferent problem

Financial-crime / onboarding: Bretton (ex-Greenlite, ~$90M), Parcha, Hummingbird, ComplyAdvantage — KYC/AML, not output audit. Legal AI: Harvey, Luminance. Media provenance: C2PA / Content Credentials, Truepic — authenticity of media, not AI-action audit (watch C2PA's new text/LLM manifests as scope-creep).

04 · Honest moat assessment

What is defensible, and what is not

Table stakes — stop pitching these as differentiation

The cryptographic pipeline itself: hash-chaining, Merkle proofs, Ed25519 signing, RFC-3161 anchoring, offline verification, post-quantum algorithm-agility. All are standardised (SCITT is now an IETF standard with a Microsoft GA implementation), open-source (Sigstore Rekor v2 is ~90% of the backend), and independently reproduced by multiple 2025–26 entrants. A competent two-engineer team can stand up the primitives from public reference code.

Genuinely defensible — concentrate here

  • Insider-proof trust model as the product. Customer-held-key encryption (content dark even to Attest) plus external anchoring (history unforgeable even by Attest) is a trust story WORM (Global Relay/Smarsh), HMAC (DeepInspect) and vendor-controlled ledgers (Azure) structurally cannot tell. No competitor found pairs customer-key encryption with anchored audit.
  • The bundle: gate + anchored record in one product. The market is split — audit vendors don't gate, guardrail vendors don't anchor. Owning both is rare.
  • UAE regulatory localisation. A UAE-qualified/eIDAS TSA anchor (our swappable ANCHOR_TSA_URL) plus DFSA/FSRA/CBUAE rule packs and regulator-legible evidence export. No named competitor is UAE-focused; the local field is advisory/GRC consultancies (MindXO, reconn) and ISO-42001 SaaS, not an evidence layer.
  • Trust / certification go-to-market. Being the vendor a UAE regulator accepts — the break-and-detect tamper test as a live sales artifact, a design-partner logo, sandbox pedigree. This is a relationship moat, not a technical one.

05 · Strategic risks

What could go wrong

  • Messaging collision with the archivers. Global Relay and Smarsh already say "tamper-evident system of record." If Attest can't crisply teach WORM ≠ externally-anchored, it sounds like a smaller version of an incumbent. This is the #1 narrative battle.
  • Fast-follower from the well-funded. Norm Ai ($267M) could bolt a cryptographic record onto its policy engine; DeepInspect could swap HMAC for asymmetric + anchoring and enter FS; Monitaur (if its signed-log claim is real) could add anchoring.
  • Hyperscaler commoditisation. Microsoft ships SCITT-compliant signing transparency (GA) and is adding offline Ed25519 receipts to its agent-governance toolkit — potentially bundled into Azure for free. Attest must not be "a hash chain you pay for."
  • Selling ahead of the rules. Until a regulator or an incident forces the bar, buyers may accept ordinary GRC logging as "good enough."
  • Mindshare capture: G42 × Credo AI. A sovereign-scale partner backing a global governance platform could own the regional "responsible AI" budget and boardroom — adjacent, not feature-for-feature, but a gorilla to route around (or partner with).
  • Category standardisation. A bespoke chain format becomes a liability once SCITT wins; VeritasChain already filed a SCITT profile.

06 · What to do about it

Positioning plan — realistic moves

  1. Re-anchor the pitch away from the hash chain.Lead with the two things rivals lack: "content we cannot read, history we cannot forge — proven to your regulator without trusting us," for UAE regulated finance. The crypto is how, not why.
  2. Win the WORM-vs-anchored argument explicitly.Put a one-page "why immutable storage isn't proof" explainer in the sales kit; make the break-and-detect tamper test a live demo. This directly neutralises Global Relay/Smarsh.
  3. Adopt SCITT interoperability, don't reinvent.Profile SCITT rather than ship a proprietary chain format — "SCITT-compatible, UAE-anchored, insider-proof, confidential content" is more defensible than "we built a hash chain," and de-risks the standardisation wave.
  4. Make UAE the moat.Wire the anchor to a UAE-qualified/eIDAS TSA; build DFSA/FSRA/CBUAE rule packs; map evidence export to Reg 10 registers and the CBUAE model-inventory/monitoring obligations. Enter via the DIFC Innovation Hub / Dubai AI Campus / FinTech Hive; use ADGM RegLab / Hub71 as the Abu Dhabi second front.
  5. Partner the governance layer, don't fight it.Be the verifiable evidence layer that Credo/IBM/Big-4/ISO-42001 GRC tools sit on top of. "They document; we prove." Turn potential budget-competitors into channels.
  6. Own the customer-key story loudly.It's the single rarest capability found and it maps exactly to the CLAUDE.md threat model (insiders on both sides). Make "even we can't read it, even we can't forge it" the headline for CISO/GC buyers.
  7. Land reference design-partners fast.A named DIFC-regulated firm that vouches for the evidence bundle is worth more than its contract — before the fast-followers and the hyperscaler bundle arrive.
  8. Keep a live watch list.Senthex, DeepInspect, Attested Intelligence, VeritasChain (nearest architecture); Norm Ai (nearest positioning + capital); IBM watsonx.governance and G42×Credo (UAE incumbency); Monitaur (could move onto our turf). Re-scan quarterly — this category went from empty to crowded in ~12 months.
